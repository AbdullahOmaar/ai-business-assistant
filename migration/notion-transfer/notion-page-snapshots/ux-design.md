## UX acceptance principles

- Mobile-first responsive behavior and clear RTL/Arabic handling where required.
- Every screen has understandable loading, empty, error, retry and success states.
- Long names and financial values remain readable on narrow screens.
- Confirm consequential financial actions; do not use AI-generated text as a substitute for validated server-side actions.
- Accessibility, keyboard/focus behavior, form validation and clear destructive-action confirmation are part of acceptance, not optional polish.
