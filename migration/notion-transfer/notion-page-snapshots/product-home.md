## Shared product rules

- Tenant/business isolation and server-side authorization are mandatory.
- Financial totals, payment status, inventory valuation, payroll posting, and report metrics must be derived/validated server-side.
- Distinguish invoices issued, cash collected, outstanding receivables/payables, and estimated profit; never double-count monetary events.
- Keep idempotency and auditable state transitions for money-moving or posting workflows.
