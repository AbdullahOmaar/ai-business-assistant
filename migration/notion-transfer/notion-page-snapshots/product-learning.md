## Learning capture rule

Capture lessons that change a product decision, test strategy, security model, financial invariant, user workflow or release process. Each entry should include date, context, source evidence, what happened, cause, lesson, follow-up owner and verification.

- Product-specific incidents, experiments and UX learnings stay with AI Business Assistant.
- Promote only proven, reusable abstractions into Aureon’s company learning by reference; do not copy the full product history.
- Keep secrets, personal customer data and sensitive payroll data out of learning records.
- Distinguish observed evidence from hypotheses. A failed experiment is useful evidence and must not be hidden.
